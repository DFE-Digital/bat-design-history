There are financial incentives available for trainee teachers, and some require a trainee to hold a degree with a minimum grade of a 2:2 to qualify. Currently in Register trainee teachers (‘Register’), degree grade is collected for UK degrees but not for non-UK degrees. We have analysed whether there was a need within the department for non-UK degree grade to be added, and collected, within the Register service.

## Analysis of non-UK degree grades

Within Register, for non-UK degrees we currently ask for the UK ENIC comparable degree level as UK ENIC provide a [Statement of Comparability](https://www.enic.org.uk/individuals/statement-of-comparability) which compares how non-UK qualifications map against the UK framework levels. We investigated UK ENIC further to see if they mapped qualifications to grades or provided an equivalence to the UK grade classification system, but they do not provide this information.

As no non-UK degree grade information is currently collected in Register, we looked at the [Apply for teacher training service](https://www.gov.uk/apply-for-teacher-training) (‘Apply’) as they already collect non-UK degree grades in a free text field. Apply had recently completed analysis on their free text field data and this identified 10 countries who follow the same grade classification system as the UK. The other countries used a mixture of systems across, and within, them.

If changes were to be made in Register, we would need to ask for both the UK equivalent grade and original grade classification for non-UK degrees as there is no recognised approach with regards to degree grade equivalency.

## Impact analysis

The policy requirements of a degree with a minimum grade of a 2:2 relate to funding eligibility. Currently, the initial teacher training (ITT) funding team already have processes in place to check and assure the eligibility criteria is met during each payment cycle.

The data collected within Register is used to generate the [Initial Teacher Training Census](https://explore-education-statistics.service.gov.uk/find-statistics/initial-teacher-training-census/releases) and the [Initial Teacher Training Performance Profiles](https://explore-education-statistics.service.gov.uk/find-statistics/initial-teacher-training-performance-profiles/releases). Within both, there are tables for degree class data, but non-UK degrees are currently recorded under the ‘Other degree type’ category meaning their class, or grade, is irrelevant.

If changes were to be made in Register, this would not impact the current funding eligibility processes, the ITT Census or the ITT Performance Profiles.

## Summary

Whilst it is possible for Register to collect non-UK degree grades, there would be no impact of doing so currently as the ITT funding team manage the payment eligibility assurance and the ITT Census and Performance Profiles do not require this information.

Therefore, unless a process or policy change is made to either of those areas, we will not be making any changes in Register currently due to a lack of need for non-UK degree grades to be held in the service.
